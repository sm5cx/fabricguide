Warum Rotwein Flecken Darken After trocknening?

Rotwein stains often look darker after drying because the pigments (anthocyanins) and tannins oxidize and bind more tightly to Stoff fibers as moisture evaporates. Heat and slow drying can speed these reactions, making the color set and harder to lift later.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
