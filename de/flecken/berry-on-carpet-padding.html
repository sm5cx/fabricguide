verhinderning Beeren Flecken from Wicking into Teppich Padding

To stop a Beeren stain from wicking into Teppich padding, act fast: blot (don’t rub), dilute with a small amount of cool water, then keep the area as dry as possible with absorbent towels and light weight on top. The goal is to pull moisture up and out of the Teppich fibers before it migrates downward into the pad.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
