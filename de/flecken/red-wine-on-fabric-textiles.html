When to Treating Red Wineflecken auf Fabrics verhindern

Stop treating a Rotwein stain when additional attempts are no longer improving the mark and your Stoff is starting to show stress (color change, fuzzing, distortion, or weakened fibers). At that point, continuing to scrub, soak, or reapply chemicals often causes more visible damage than the remaining stain.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
