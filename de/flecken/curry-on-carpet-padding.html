Wie man Diagnose a Curry Fleck auf Teppich Padding

To diagnose a Curry stain in Teppich padding, lift the Teppich edge and check the pad directly under the spill for yellow/orange pigment, oily darkening, and dampness. If the padding is discolored, still smells like Curry after drying, or feels stiff or greasy, the stain has likely penetrated and may need deeper cleaning or pad replacement.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don’t flood the area with water or cleaner “to see what happens”; extra liquid can push pigment and oil deeper into the padding.
  • Don’t scrub aggressively from the top; friction can den Fleck verteilen and drive residue through the backing.
  • Don’t rely on heat (hair dryer on hot, iron, steam) during diagnosis; heat can set pigments and oxidize oils, making discoloration harder to remove.
  • Don’t ignore faint staining underneath. Even light yellowing on the pad can later wick back up and reappear on the carpet face.
  • Don’t mix multiple strong chemicals while testing; you can create damage or odors that complicate assessment.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
