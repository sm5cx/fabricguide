Should I Photograph a Wein Fleck Before trocknen reinigening a Seide Item?

Yes—take clear photos of the Wein stain before you send a Seide item to the dry cleaner. Quick documentation preserves what the stain looked like, can help you and the cleaner discuss treatment, and gives you a record if questions come up later.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
