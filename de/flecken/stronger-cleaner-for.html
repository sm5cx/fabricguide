Questions to Ask Before Using Stronger reinigeners

Before stepping up to a stronger cleaner, confirm the stain type, the surface or Stoff’s tolerance, and what you’ve already tried. A short check can prevent setting the stain, causing color loss, or damaging finishes while still getting better results.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
