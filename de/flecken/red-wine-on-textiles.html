Differentiating Residue from Remaining Pigment in Rotwein Flecken

If the mark looks colored (pink, purple, or brown) and stays visible when the Stoff is fully dry, you’re likely seeing remaining pigment. If it looks dull, cloudy, stiff, or slightly sticky and changes appearance between wet and dry, it’s more likely cleaning residue or a texture change rather than Wein color.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
