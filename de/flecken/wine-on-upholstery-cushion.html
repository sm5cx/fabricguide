Safe Transport of Wein-Flecked Polstermöbel Kissens

To safely transport a Wein-stained Polstermöbel Kissen, blot any surface wetness, isolate the stained area so it can’t touch other fabrics, and keep the Kissen as flat and still as possible during transit. Bag or wrap it with clean, non-dyed materials and take it to a professional promptly to limit stain setting and odor.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
