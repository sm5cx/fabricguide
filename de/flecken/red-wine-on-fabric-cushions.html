First Steps for Rotwein Flecken auf Sofa Kissen Covers

Blot (don’t rub) the Rotwein immediately with a clean, absorbent cloth to pull up as much liquid as possible, then keep blotting with small amounts of cool water if you have it. If you can’t fully clean it right away, leave the cover to air-dry and plan to treat it properly as soon as you can.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
