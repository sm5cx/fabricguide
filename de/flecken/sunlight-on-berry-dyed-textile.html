Impact of Sunlight auf Beeren Pigments in Textils

Sunlight, especially UV, can change Beeren pigments in textiles by fading, shifting, or unevenly altering the color over time. The same exposure can also weaken some fibers, so a Stoff may lose both color strength and overall integrity if it sits in direct sun for long periods.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
