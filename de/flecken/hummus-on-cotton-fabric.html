Warum Hummus Flecken Feel Stiff After trocknening?

Dried Hummus stains often feel stiff on Baumwolle because the stain leaves behind a mix of oils, proteins, and starches that dry into a film and bind to the fibers. Heat and time can set that residue, making the Stoff feel crunchy until the buildup is rehydrated and lifted out with proper cleaning.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
