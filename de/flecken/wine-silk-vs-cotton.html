Understanding the Risks of Wein Flecken auf Seide vs. Baumwolle

Wein stains are generally riskier on Seide than on Baumwolle because Seide’s delicate fibers and finishes can be damaged by water, rubbing, and common stain removers. Baumwolle typically tolerates gentler washing and spot treatment better, so you usually have more room to act quickly and successfully.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
