Removing Hummus Flecken from Stoff Kleidung

Rinse the area from the back with cool water, then gently work in a small amount of liquid laundry detergent and rinse again. If the stain spread after dish soap, focus on thorough rinsing first, then treat the whole affected area evenly before washing.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
