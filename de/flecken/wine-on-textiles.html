Determining the Removability of a Wein Fleck auf Stoffs

Most Rotwein stains on fabrics are still removable, especially if they’re fresh or were only rinsed with water. A stain is more likely to be stubborn if it has been heat-dried, left for a long time, or has already set into the fibers, but you can often still lighten it with careful treatment.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
