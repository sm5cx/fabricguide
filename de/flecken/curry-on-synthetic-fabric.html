Removing Curry Flecken from Synthetik Stoff

Blot off excess Curry, then flush the back of the stain with cold water and work in a small amount of liquid dish soap. Rinse, then wash with detergent in the coolest water the care label allows, air-drying until the stain is fully gone.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
