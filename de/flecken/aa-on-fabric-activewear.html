Triage an Açaí Fleck auf Sportbekleidung

Blot the açaí stain right away, then flush it with cold water from the back of the Stoff to push the pigment out. Work a small amount of liquid laundry detergent into the stain, rinse, and air-dry until you’re sure it’s gone (heat can set what you can’t see yet).

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don’t use hot water. Heat can set berry pigments into synthetic performance fabrics.
  • Don’t rub aggressively. Friction can drive pigment deeper and may damage stretchy fibers.
  • Don’t machine-dry “to see if it’s gone.” Dryer heat can lock in faint remaining discoloration.
  • Don’t mix cleaners. Avoid combining bleach types or using multiple products at once.
  • Don’t ignore the care label. Some activewear finishes and elastane blends need gentler products and cooler temperatures.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
