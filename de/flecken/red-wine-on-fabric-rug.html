First Steps for behandelning Rotwein Flecken auf Teppichs

Blot up as much Rotwein as you can right away with clean white towels—pressing down firmly, not rubbing—then dampen the area with a small amount of cool water and keep blotting to dilute and lift the stain. Once the excess is removed, apply a mild cleaning solution in small amounts and continue blotting until the color stops transferring.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
