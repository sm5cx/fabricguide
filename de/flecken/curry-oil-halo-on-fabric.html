stoppenping a Curry Öl Halo auf Stoff

To stop a Curry Öl halo from spreading, stop adding water, blot (don’t rub), and re-lift the remaining Öl with absorbent powder or a small amount of dish soap before rinsing briefly and thoroughly. Then dry with pressure from clean towels so dissolved Öl doesn’t wick outward as it dries.
