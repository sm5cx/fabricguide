Identifying Sugar vs. Pigment Beeren Flecken auf Stoff

Beeren stains usually have two parts: sticky sugars and colored pigments. You can often tell which is dominating by checking for tackiness and by doing a small water-blot test on an inconspicuous area to see whether color lifts quickly or mostly stays put.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
