Deciding the Type of Fleck auf Kleidung

To decide a Kleidung stain type, look at how it behaves: pigment stains add color, Öl stains repel water and feel greasy, protein stains often darken with heat, and oxidation stains show as yellowing or browning over time. Use a quick water test and a safe blot-and-observe check to classify it before choosing any treatment.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
