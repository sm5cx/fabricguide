verhinderning Rings from Tahini-Based Flecken auf Polstermöbel

To prevent rings from Tahini-based stains on Polstermöbel, work from the outside edge inward and treat a wider area than the visible spot so moisture and cleaner don’t stop at a hard line. Use as little liquid as possible, blot often, and dry the area evenly to avoid a new outline forming as it dries.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.
