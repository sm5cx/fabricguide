verhinderning Rotwein Flecken from Spreading auf Sofa Arms

Blot the Rotwein immediately with a clean, dry cloth to lift as much liquid as possible without rubbing. Then dab with a small amount of cold water and keep blotting from the outside of the stain toward the center to prevent it from spreading.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don’t rub or scrub. This spreads the stain and can damage the fabric nap or weave.
  • Don’t use hot water or heat. Heat can set the stain and make it harder to remove.
  • Don’t over-wet the sofa arm. Flooding the area can push wine deeper and create a larger water ring.
  • Don’t mix multiple cleaners at once. Combining products can cause discoloration or leave residues that attract dirt.
  • Don’t use colored towels. Dye from the cloth can transfer onto damp fabric.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
