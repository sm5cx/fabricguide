Understanding Fleck Appearance auf Baumwolle Stoff

Some stains look lighter when wet because water changes how light passes through the stain and the Baumwolle fibers, temporarily masking color. As the Stoff dries, the remaining residue concentrates and the fibers return to their normal texture, making the stain appear darker again.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
