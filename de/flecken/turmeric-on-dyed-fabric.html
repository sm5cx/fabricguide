Wie man Test Colorfastness Before behandelning a Kurkuma Fleck

Before treating a Kurkuma stain on dyed Stoff, do a quick colorfastness test so your stain remover or water won’t pull out or bleed the dye. Test a hidden spot with the same liquid you plan to use, then check the cloth and the Stoff for any color transfer before you proceed.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
