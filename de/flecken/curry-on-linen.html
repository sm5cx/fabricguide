Why Are Curry Flecken Harder to behandeln auf Leinen Than Baumwolle?

Curry stains can be harder to remove from Leinen than Baumwolle because Leinen’s fiber structure can trap oily, pigmented spices more stubbornly and shows discoloration more readily. Leinen is also more likely to have finishes or dyes that react poorly to aggressive stain removal, increasing the risk of fading or uneven color change.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
