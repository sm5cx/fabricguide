Was tun After Hydrogen Peroxide Lightens Your Baumwolle Bluse

If hydrogen peroxide has lightened your Baumwolle Bluse, stop treating the area and rinse it thoroughly with cool water to remove any remaining peroxide. Then let it air-dry and assess the color change in good light; true bleaching won’t “wash out,” but you may be able to reduce the contrast or re-dye the Kleidungsstück.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
