Potential Bleaching auf Baumwolle Hemd from Dish Soap

Dish soap usually doesn’t “bleach” Baumwolle the way chlorine bleach does, but it can leave a lighter-looking patch by stripping dye, leaving residue, or causing uneven rinsing—especially after spot-scrubbing a stain. If the area is still pale after a thorough rinse and wash, the color loss may be permanent, but you can often reduce the contrast and prevent it from spreading.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
