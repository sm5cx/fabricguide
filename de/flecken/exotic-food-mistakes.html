Irreversible Mistakes to Avoid with Exotic Lebensmittel Flecken

With exotic Lebensmittel stains, the most irreversible damage usually comes from using the wrong chemistry (like heat on protein, or strong bleach on dyes) or locking the stain in by rubbing and over-wetting. Avoid permanent setting by identifying what kind of stain you’re dealing with, treating gently in the right order, and testing for colorfastness before you use any stronger method.
