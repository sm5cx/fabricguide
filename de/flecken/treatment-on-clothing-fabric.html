Determining Wann Halt Fleck behandelnment

Stop stain treatment when the stain is no longer improving after a few careful attempts, or when the Stoff starts showing stress (fading, roughness, stretching, or weakened fibers). At that point, switch to gentle care (rinse, air-dry, and reassess) or consider professional cleaning to avoid setting the stain or damaging the Kleidungsstück.

Sofortmaßnahmen

  1. Sofort abtupfen. Ein sauberes Tuch verwenden to überschüssige Flüssigkeit aufnehmen.

Das sollten Sie vermeiden

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains dauerhaft machenly.

Hinweise für häufige Situationen

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
