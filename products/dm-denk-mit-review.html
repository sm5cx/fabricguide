dm Denkmit Fleckensalz — Best Budget Stain Remover

dm Denkmit Fleckensalz matched the market leader with Stiftung Warentest Grade 2.4 (Good) at just €1.45 — less than half the price of Sil [S1]. This dm store-brand stain salt delivers virtually identical performance to Sil 1 für Alles in lab testing. The only catch: it's exclusively available at dm stores. If you shop at dm regularly, this is objectively the smartest buy.

  • ✅ Same test grade as the market leader — Grade 2.4 matches Sil exactly [S1]
  • ✅ Best price-performance ratio — €1.45 for quality that costs €3.50+ from name brands
  • ✅ Good fabric and color protection — Grade 1.9, safe for colored fabrics

Weaknesses (Honest Assessment)

  • ❌ dm exclusive — only available at dm stores and dm.de, not at Rossmann, Rewe, or Amazon
  • ❌ Slightly lower stain removal score — 2.4 vs Sil's 2.3 (marginal difference, unlikely noticeable)
  • ❌ Less well-known — store brands inspire less confidence (despite matching test results)
  • ❌ Same soaking requirement as all powder formats — 30–60 minutes minimum

Who Should Buy Denkmit?

Buy Denkmit if: You shop at dm (Germany's largest drugstore chain) and want the best value stain remover with lab-proven results. There's no rational reason to pay more than double for Sil when you have dm access.

Buy Sil instead if: You don't have a dm nearby and don't want to order online, or if you prefer the peace of mind of the name-brand test winner.

How to Use

  1. Dissolve 1–2 tablespoons in warm water (30–40°C)
  2. Soak the stained garment for 30–60 minutes (up to 6 hours for stubborn stains)
  3. Wash at the highest temperature the care label allows
  4. Machine boost: Add 1 tablespoon to the drum with your regular detergent
