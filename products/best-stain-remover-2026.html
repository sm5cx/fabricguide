Why Sil Won

Sil 1 für Alles was the only product to achieve top ratings in both stain removal and fabric protection. Key advantages over competitors:

  • Balanced performance: Excellent stain removal without color damage
  • Universal application: Effective on all tested stain types (red wine, blood, grease)
  • Color protection: Protects fabric colors better than Vanish and Ariel
  • Fair pricing: €2.49 per 500ml — mid-range pricing segment

Limitation: Sil's powder format requires pre-soaking and is less convenient than spray alternatives for instant spot treatment.

Stiftung Warentest Methodology

The evaluation is based on standardized testing protocols:

  • Test scope: 20 universal stain removers + 3 ultrasonic pens
  • Stain types: Red wine, blood, grease, cocoa, tea — the most common household stains
  • Fabric types: Cotton, polyester, blends in various colors
  • Wash temperatures: 30°C, 40°C, and 60°C (86°F, 104°F, 140°F)
  • Rating criteria: Stain removal (60%), color protection (30%), handling (10%)

Source: Stiftung Warentest, test.de, 2024

How to Use Sil Optimally

  1. Fresh stains: Dissolve 1-2 tsp powder in cold water, soak stain for 15 minutes
  2. Set-in stains: Make paste with powder + minimal water, apply to stain, let sit 30 minutes
  3. Regular laundry: Add 1 scoop to detergent for boost
  4. White fabrics: Wash at 60°C (140°F) for optimal performance

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sil better than Vanish?

According to Stiftung Warentest, yes. Sil achieved grade 2.4 (good) while Vanish scored 2.9 (satisfactory). The main difference: Vanish removes stains well but damages fabric colors more than Sil.

Does Sil work on all stain types?

Sil is designed as a universal stain remover for most household stains. For very specific stains (gum, wax), specialized products may be more effective.

How long does Sil last?

Unopened: 3 years. After opening, store in a dry place — powder can absorb moisture and clump together.

Is Sil available internationally?

Sil is a Henkel brand with international presence, but product formulations may vary by market. Check local availability through Henkel distributors.
