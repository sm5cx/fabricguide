Best Stain Removers Compared — Independent Test Results

Sil 1 für Alles and dm Denkmit share the top spot with Grade 2.4 (Good) in Stiftung Warentest testing [S1]. Denkmit costs less than half the price (€1.45 vs €3.50). Vanish Oxi Action (Grade 2.9) trades some effectiveness for spray convenience. For most households, a powder stain salt plus optional spray covers all scenarios.
