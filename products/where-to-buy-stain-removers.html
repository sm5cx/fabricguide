The cheapest way to get a top-rated stain remover is dm Denkmit Fleckensalz at €1.45 — it earned the same Stiftung Warentest Grade 2.4 as the pricier Sil [S1]. Sil 1 für Alles is cheapest at dm (~€3.45) and Rossmann (~€3.49). Avoid Amazon for standard products — prices are typically 30–40% higher than in-store. All three brands are widely available across Germany.

Online Shopping Options

dm Online Shop (dm.de)

Same prices as in-store. Free pickup in-store or home delivery (usually free above €49). Only place to get Denkmit online.

Rossmann Online

Similar to dm — in-store prices, home delivery or store pickup. Good alternative if no dm nearby.

Amazon.de

Largest selection including bulk packs and specialty products. Prices are typically higher than drugstores, but Prime delivery is convenient. Best for: bulk buying, international shipping, or hard-to-find specialty products.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Watch for 20% sales: dm and Rossmann regularly discount laundry products — stock up when they do
  • Buy store brands: dm Denkmit and Rossmann Domol offer comparable quality at lower prices
  • Powder > Spray for value: Powder formats give more treatments per euro than spray bottles
  • Stain salt lasts: It's shelf-stable for years — buying extra during sales is risk-free

For International Readers

Outside Germany? Sil and Vanish are available via Amazon.de with international shipping to most EU countries. In Austria and Switzerland, these products are sold in local drugstores (dm, Müller, Coop). For the UK, Vanish is widely available; Sil may require import.

For country-specific alternatives, check your local consumer testing organization — many countries have their own stain remover ratings.
