Sil vs Vanish: Which Stain Remover Actually Wins?

Sil is the better stain remover. Stiftung Warentest gave Sil 1 für Alles Grade 2.4 (Good) versus Vanish Oxi Action's Grade 2.7 (Satisfactory) — a full grade tier apart [S1]. Sil also scored better on color protection (1.8 vs 2.3) and fabric safety (1.8 vs 2.1). The chemistry tells the story: Sil combines oxygen bleach with enzymes for broad-spectrum stain removal, while Vanish relies primarily on oxygen bleach alone. At €0.14 per wash vs €0.50, Sil delivers better results at roughly a third of the cost.

Active Ingredients: Why Performance Differs

The performance gap comes down to formulation philosophy [S2] [S3]:

Sil 1 für Alles — Enzyme + Bleach Hybrid

Sil uses oxygen bleach (sodium percarbonate) combined with protease and amylase enzymes. Proteases break down protein-based stains (blood, egg, milk, grass, sweat), while amylases target starch residues. The enzyme action during the 30–60 minute soak gives Sil its edge on organic stains — enzymes need contact time to work.

Vanish Oxi Action — Oxygen Bleach Forward

Vanish relies primarily on higher concentrations of sodium percarbonate (oxygen bleach) with surfactants. This bleach-heavy approach is more effective on color-based stains — wine, berry, curry, and dye transfers — where oxidation breaks the chromophore molecules that cause visible color. Less enzyme content means weaker protein stain performance.

Stain-by-Stain: Use Sil for… Use Vanish for…

Key takeaway: Protein stains (anything from a living organism) respond better to Sil's enzyme formula. Color stains (plant pigments, dyes) respond well to Vanish's oxidizing power. For stains that have both protein and color components (like grass), Sil's combined approach gives it the edge.

Verdict: Sil Is the Better Choice for Most Households

Sil wins this comparison. It scores higher in independent testing (Grade 2.4 vs 2.7), protects colors better (1.8 vs 2.3), is safer for fabrics (1.8 vs 2.1), and costs roughly a third per treatment [S1]. For the vast majority of household stain situations — food, grass, blood, sweat, everyday spills — Sil delivers superior results backed by Stiftung Warentest data.

Vanish has a narrow convenience advantage: its spray format is useful for upholstery you can't submerge and for immediate spot treatment when you can't soak. But this convenience comes at 3× the cost per treatment and measurably weaker performance.

Our recommendation: Start with Sil as your primary stain remover. If you find yourself needing a spray for emergencies or furniture, add Vanish as a secondary tool. But Sil should be the foundation of any stain removal toolkit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sil better than Vanish for stain removal?

Yes, overall. Sil scored Grade 2.4 vs Vanish's 2.7 in independent testing [S1]. Sil is especially stronger on protein stains (blood, egg, grass) due to its enzyme content. Vanish performs better on color-based stains like wine and berry, and wins on convenience with its spray format.

What is the price per wash for Sil vs Vanish?

Sil costs approximately €0.12–0.18 per treatment (500g pack at ~€3.50, yielding 25–30 soaking treatments). Vanish Oxi Action spray costs approximately €0.40–0.60 per application (500ml at ~€5.95, yielding 10–15 spray treatments). Sil delivers roughly 3× more treatments per euro [S2] [S3].

Can I use Sil and Vanish together?

Don't mix them in the same solution. You can use them sequentially: spray Vanish for immediate pre-treatment, then soak in Sil solution before machine washing. This two-step approach can tackle stains that have both color and protein components.

Which stain remover is safer for colored fabrics?

Sil is measurably safer. It scored 1.8 for color protection in Stiftung Warentest testing, compared to Vanish's 2.3 [S1]. Sil's balanced enzyme approach is gentler on fabric dyes than Vanish's higher oxygen bleach concentration.
