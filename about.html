About Fabric Care Guide

Fabric Care Guide is an independent resource for evidence-based stain removal and fabric care. We are not affiliated with any brand or manufacturer. Our 108+ stain guides and product reviews are based on Stiftung Warentest data, ISO textile testing standards, and practical verification. No ads, no affiliate links, no sponsored content — just honest, useful information backed by science and testing data.

Our Mission

Stain removal advice online is dominated by myths, clickbait, and affiliate-driven product recommendations. We exist to provide something different: accurate, evidence-based guidance that actually helps you save your clothes and textiles.

Every recommendation on this site comes from verifiable sources — primarily Stiftung Warentest (Germany's most trusted consumer testing organization, government-backed since 1964) and published textile chemistry research. When we say a product works, we cite the test grade and source so you can verify it yourself.

What We Offer

  • 108+ stain removal guides — organized by stain type, fabric, and urgency
  • Product reviews — based on Stiftung Warentest independent lab testing, not manufacturer claims
  • Bilingual content — full site available in English and German
  • Myth-busting — we test and debunk popular stain removal myths
  • Practical tools — equipment guides, prevention tips, emergency procedures

No Affiliate Links — Here's Why

We don't earn commissions when you buy products we recommend. Zero.

Most stain removal websites earn money through affiliate links — they get paid when you click through and purchase. This creates a fundamental conflict of interest: they're incentivized to recommend the most expensive products, not the best ones.

When we recommend dm Denkmit over a more expensive brand (same test grade, half the price), it's because the data supports it — not because someone is paying us. Our recommendations are based purely on independent test results, fabric safety, and value for money.

Our Editorial Standards

Evidence Hierarchy

We follow a strict evidence hierarchy for every claim we make:

  1. 🥇 Gold Standard: Stiftung Warentest results and ISO textile testing standards — independent, laboratory-verified data
  2. 🥈 Silver Standard: Manufacturer specifications and published textile chemistry research
  3. 🥉 Bronze Standard: Practical testing and verified consumer experience

Every recommendation clearly indicates its evidence level. Read our full testing methodology for details.

Editorial Process

Our editorial team reviews and verifies every guide before publication. Each piece of content goes through a multi-step process:

  1. Research — source identification, test data collection, and literature review
  2. Writing — clear, actionable instructions grounded in chemistry
  3. Fact-checking — every claim verified against original sources
  4. Practical verification — methods tested on actual fabrics
  5. Quarterly review — content updated when new test data is published

Honest Negatives

We believe trust comes from honesty, not from only saying positive things. Every product review includes a clear "Weaknesses" section. If the test winner requires 60 minutes of soaking, we say so. If a budget product matches the market leader, we recommend the cheaper option.

Our Expertise

Our team brings together years of experience in textile care research, consumer product analysis, and practical stain removal testing. We work with:

  • Stiftung Warentest data — decades of standardized product testing under laboratory conditions
  • ISO textile standards — international standards for fabric care and colorfastness
  • Textile chemistry research — published scientific literature on stain-fiber interactions
  • Real-world testing — practical verification of methods on actual garments and fabrics
