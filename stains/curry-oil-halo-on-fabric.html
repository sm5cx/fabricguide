Stopping a Curry Oil Halo on Fabric

To stop a curry oil halo from spreading, stop adding water, blot (don’t rub), and re-lift the remaining oil with absorbent powder or a small amount of dish soap before rinsing briefly and thoroughly. Then dry with pressure from clean towels so dissolved oil doesn’t wick outward as it dries.

Steps to Take Right Away

  1. Blot immediately. Use a clean cloth to absorb excess liquid.

What Not to Do

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains permanently.

Notes for Common Situations

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
