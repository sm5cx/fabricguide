Does Water Spread Wine Stains on Upholstery?

Yes—water can spread a red wine stain on upholstery if you pour or scrub it in, because the liquid can carry dye deeper and wider through the fabric. Used sparingly and with blotting, water can also help dilute what’s left and lift it into a towel without enlarging the stain.

For the general approach, see: wine on upholstery fabric.

Steps to Take Right Away

  1. Blot immediately. Use a clean cloth to absorb excess liquid.

What Not to Do

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains permanently.
