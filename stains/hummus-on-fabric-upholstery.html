Safely Blotting Hummus Stains from Upholstery

Lift off any hummus solids first, then blot (don’t rub) with a clean, absorbent cloth to pull oil and moisture out of the fabric. If needed, use a small amount of mild, water-based solution after testing colorfastness, and keep blotting until the stain lightens.

Step-by-step: Safely blot hummus from fabric upholstery

  1. Scoop off excess. Use a dull edge to lift hummus solids from the surface without pressing them deeper into the fabric.
  2. Blot immediately. Press a clean, absorbent cloth straight down on the stain, then lift. Repeat with fresh sections of cloth to absorb as much as possible.
  3. Work from the outside in. Blot around the edges first, then toward the center to reduce spreading.
  4. Test colorfastness. On an inconspicuous spot, dab a tiny amount of your chosen cleaning solution with a cloth and blot dry. If color transfers or the fabric changes, stop and use only dry blotting.
  5. Apply a small amount of mild solution (if safe). Lightly dampen a cloth with water or a mild, water-based cleaner (do not soak the upholstery). Dab onto the stain and blot.
  6. Alternate damp blotting and dry blotting. After each small application, switch to a dry cloth to pull moisture and residue back out of the fabric.
  7. Rinse by blotting. If you used any cleaner, blot with a cloth lightly dampened with plain water, then blot dry to remove leftover cleaner that can attract dirt.
  8. Dry thoroughly. Keep blotting with a dry cloth until barely damp, then let the area air-dry. Once dry, gently blot or lift the nap with a clean cloth to restore texture.

What to avoid

  • Don’t rub or scrub. This can push hummus deeper and fuzz or distort fabric fibers.
  • Don’t soak the cushion. Over-wetting can spread the stain and leave moisture trapped in the padding.
  • Don’t use colored or linty cloths. Dye transfer and lint can make cleanup harder.
  • Don’t skip the colorfastness test. Even mild solutions can shift or lift dye on some fabrics.
  • Don’t blast with heat. Heat while residue is present can make staining harder to remove.

Notes for common situations

Immediate treatment of the stain is critical. The sooner you remove solids and start blotting, the less time the oils have to bond with the fabric.

Use a clean, absorbent cloth for blotting. Rotate to a fresh, dry area often so you’re lifting hummus out instead of re-depositing it.

Test fabric colorfastness before applying any solution. If you see dye on the cloth or a visible change, stick to dry blotting and consider getting professional help for that upholstery fabric.

Educational guidance only. Always test on an inconspicuous area first.
