Understanding the Darkening of Berry Smoothie Stains on Fabric

Berry smoothie stains can look lighter when wet and then darken as they air-dry because the colored compounds concentrate as water evaporates and can bind more firmly to the fabric over time. Exposure to oxygen and light during drying can also shift the color, making the stain appear deeper or more set.

Steps to Take Right Away

  1. Blot immediately. Use a clean cloth to absorb excess liquid.

What Not to Do

  • Don't use hot water initially. Heat can set many stains permanently.

Notes for Common Situations

If stain is fresh: Speed matters most - treat immediately for best results.
