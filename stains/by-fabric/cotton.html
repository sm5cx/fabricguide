💪 Cotton Advantages

Cotton is forgiving, but technique still matters:

  • Heat tolerance: Can use hot water (except for protein stains)
  • Chemical resistance: Handles most stain removers safely
  • Scrubbing tolerance: More durable than silk/wool
  • Bleach safe: White cotton can handle oxygen bleach

⚠️ Still test any treatment on hidden area first!

Educational guidance only. Always test on an inconspicuous area first.
