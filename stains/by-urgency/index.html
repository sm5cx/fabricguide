How Urgent Is Your Stain?

Time is the single biggest factor in stain removal success. A fresh red wine stain treated within 60 seconds has a 90%+ removal rate — the same stain left overnight drops to under 50%. Use this guide to understand how urgently your stain needs attention and find the right treatment guide.

🔴 Act NOW (0–30 min) 🟡 Act Today (30 min–2 hr) 🟢 Can Wait (2 hr+) ⚫ Already Set

🚨 Universal First Response

Whatever your stain, these 4 steps always apply:

  1. Stop the source — prevent the stain from spreading
  2. Blot, never rub — rubbing pushes stains deeper into fibres
  3. Cold water first — hot water sets most protein and tannin stains
  4. Test before treating — always check a hidden area first
