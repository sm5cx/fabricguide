🚨 Wine Spill Emergency Protocol

First 60 seconds are critical:

  1. Blot immediately — soak up as much wine as possible
  2. Cold water only — hot water sets wine stains permanently
  3. Work from outside in — prevent spreading
  4. Never scrub or rub — pushes wine deeper into fibers
